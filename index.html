"I don't have time for this"

"Ghostwriters have burned me before"
Most ghostwriters work in isolation for months. We work in Google Docs. You see every chapter as it's written. And we deliver in 7 days.

Good. You don't need to be. We extract your natural voice through conversation. The book reads like you dictated it.
"Will it actually sound like me?"
Every chapter uses your stories and examples. You approve the outline. You approve the draft. Nothing goes final without your sign-off.

The book builds one. Amazon's search algorithm surfaces books based on categories and keywords. We optimize both.

We finish. Your only dependency is one 30-minute call. After that, we carry it to completion. Day 7 = book is live.

Amazon's nonfiction sweet spot is 12K-25K. Long enough to deliver value. Short enough that readers finish it.

You approve every milestone. If unsatisfied at any point, full refund. No questions asked.
"I don't know what my book would be about"
